# ENE_business_survey_block_sample
Impact of COVID-19 on GVC's of Peru

Peru offers an interesting case to explore the potential ex-ante economic effects of the COVID-19 shock. Informality exceeds 70 percent of the labor force, with higher levels of informality concentrated in contact-intensive activities such as commerce, transport, restaurants and tourism. Additionally, in spite of stringent mitigation policies in place since March 2020, Peru is one of the countries in Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) with the highest rates of infected by million inhabitants, whereas the rate of contagion and deaths as well as the intensity of the mitigation measures also varies significantly across regions . Finally, given the specialization of the Peruvian economy in primary production and tourism, local firms participating in global value chains (GVCs) are likely to be impacted by the COVID-19 negative shock on import demand from China and international travel.

Drawing on the National Business Survey (Encuesta Nacional de Empresas), National Household Survey (Encuesta Nacional de Hogares), National Input-Output matrix, World Bank’s trade and financial vulnerability data this report finds that ex-ante between 40 to 46 percent of Peru’s labor force -depending on the sample- and 18 percent of its Gross Domestic Product (GDP) could be exposed to the combined demand and supply shocks caused by COVID-19.
